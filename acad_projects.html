I.

Virtual Carving with Haptics - Final Year Project

My NTU Final Year Project, Virtual Carving Using the Desktop Haptic Device 3DSystems Geomagic Touch (College of Computing and Data Science, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Alexei Sourin). I built a virtual carving system in Unity that couples the Geomagic Touch haptic stylus with the SOFA physics engine through SofaUnity, with real-time 3D stylus tracking, button-triggered carving, and force feedback from a surface-normal penalty model, integrated via the HapticsDirect.dll native wrapper.

To isolate the effect of force feedback itself, I tested it against a working 3D mouse-and-keyboard carving baseline in a counterbalanced within-subjects user study (N=12). With haptics, carving accuracy improved by about 28% and mental workload (NASA-TLX) dropped by about 27%, both significant at p < 0.001 with large effect sizes (d = 1.45 and 1.88).
